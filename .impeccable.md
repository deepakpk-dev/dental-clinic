# Aura Dental Care — Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
**Primary:** Adults aged 22–55 in Ottapalam and surrounding Palakkad district, Kerala, searching on a phone for "dentist near me." Often anxious about dental work, often comparing 2–3 clinics before making contact. Mostly use English on the web even if Malayalam at home. Many will arrive via Google Maps or Google search, not direct.

**Secondary:** Parents booking for children (children's dentistry is a meaningful share of services), and the older generation (50+) booking implants.

**Context of use:** Pulled out of a pocket on a 4G/5G phone, often standing or commuting, often after deciding "I should finally get this looked at." Decision must happen in under 90 seconds.

**Job to be done:** "Reassure me this clinic is legitimate, modern, and gentle — then let me lock in an appointment without phoning a stranger."

### Brand Personality
Three words: **warm**, **considered**, **modern-Keralan**.

- Voice: a knowledgeable older sister, not a hospital administrator. Direct but never cold. Mildly poetic on the headlines, plainspoken in the body.
- Emotional goals: lower the patient's heart rate. Replace "I'm dreading this" with "okay, I can do this."
- The brand is unapologetically purple/lavender — not a tech-startup gradient but a Keralan textile lavender. The site should feel closer to a boutique hotel than a hospital website.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** Light. Patients open the site late at night when they can't sleep because of tooth pain — a dark glowy "trading dashboard" aesthetic would feel hostile and cold. A soft cream/lavender canvas reads "calm."
- **References (positive):** Apostrophe (skincare e-commerce), Hims/Hers homepage editorial sensibility, Aesop's typographic restraint, Dieline-style boutique product packaging — but warmer and more local. Indian wedding card / Keralan textile color sensibility.
- **Anti-references:** Generic medical/hospital sites (clipart icons, blue gradients, stock photo of doctor pointing at a clipboard). SaaS landing pages. Tech-bro purple-to-blue gradients. Glassmorphism dashboards. Overly playful pediatric clinics with cartoons everywhere.
- **Differentiator:** A clinic site that reads like a magazine. Editorial typography, intentional asymmetry, restraint in color, single moments of warmth.

### Design Principles
1. **Calm over impressive.** Every animation, color, and word should lower anxiety. If a flourish doesn't reassure, it's removed.
2. **Editorial, not corporate.** Treat the page like a long-form magazine spread — generous margins, mixed type, asymmetric grids — not a templated dashboard with same-sized cards.
3. **One moment per section.** Each section earns one memorable detail (a typographic flourish, a single soft motion, an unexpected layout). No section is a wall of polish — restraint creates the perception of quality.
4. **Local but global.** The work should feel rooted in Kerala (the lavender, the warmth, the directness) while meeting global production standards (Lighthouse 95+, real typography, accessible color contrast).
5. **Mobile is the canvas.** Desktop is the bonus. Every type ramp, every spacing decision, every motion is auditioned at 390px first.

## Notes for impeccable runs

- v1 ships only the home page + booking form. Other routes (`/services`, `/about`, `/gallery`, `/contact`) are intentional stubs.
- Real photography and real doctor names are not yet available — placeholder SVG illustrations and fictional names (Dr. Anjali Menon, Dr. Rohan Krishnan) are used in `components/sections/doctors.tsx` and `public/images/`.
- WhatsApp handoff is the booking mechanism (no calendar integration in v1).
- Reduced motion is required and currently respected throughout.
