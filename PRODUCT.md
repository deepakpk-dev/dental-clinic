# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People and families in and around Ottapalam who need a trusted dentist, often while comparing clinics on a phone and sometimes while feeling anxious about treatment. They need to understand the clinic's services, feel reassured about the experience, and book or call without friction.

## Product Purpose

Present Aura Dental Care as a modern, dependable local clinic and turn confidence into appointments. The site should make the quality of the environment and approach tangible, explain the available care in plain language, and move visitors naturally toward WhatsApp booking, a phone call, or a visit to Asco Plaza.

## Brand Personality

Warm, composed, exact. Aura should feel like thoughtful contemporary hospitality applied to dentistry: calm enough for an anxious patient, polished enough to signal modern care, and human enough to feel rooted in Ottapalam.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic white-and-teal healthcare templates, luxury-for-luxury's-sake black-and-gold styling, faux editorial magazines, decorative medical icon grids, cartoon teeth, sci-fi clinic imagery, glassmorphism, fabricated statistics, and exaggerated clinical promises.

## Design Principles

1. Make reassurance visible. Show a calm, credible environment and explain what happens next.
2. Let care feel local. Keep the address, hours, phone, WhatsApp flow, and Ottapalam context easy to find.
3. Use confidence, not claims. Prefer clear specifics and real operational details over unverified ratings or inflated numbers.
4. Treat booking as the primary journey. Every section should make the next step feel simpler, never more pressured.
5. Make premium mean considered. Strong art direction, typography, motion, and materials should support trust rather than decorate it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain strong text contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks and labels, 44px minimum interactive targets, complete mobile navigation behavior, useful alternative text, and a reduced-motion experience. Do not rely on color alone to communicate meaning.
